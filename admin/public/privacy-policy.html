How long we keep it

  • Account and vehicle data: while your account is open
  • Booking, payment and invoice records: [RETENTION PERIOD — confirm against UAE tax law, commonly 5 years], because we are required to retain financial records
  • Uploaded documents and photographs: until you delete them or close your account

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to restrict or object to how we use it. To exercise any of these, email support@carnanny.app.

Deleting your account: open the app, go to Profile, and tap Delete my account — or follow the steps at carnanny.app/delete-account.html. This erases your personal details, documents, photographs and messages. Past bookings and invoices are kept as anonymous records that no longer identify you, because we are obliged to retain financial records.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes and are never recoverable. Uploaded documents are stored privately and are readable only through short-lived links issued to you.

Children

Car Nanny is intended for vehicle owners and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data, we will update this page and revise the date above. Significant changes will be notified in the app.

Questions or complaints? Contact support@carnanny.app. You also have the right to complain to the UAE Data Office.
