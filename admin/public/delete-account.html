Delete your Car Nanny account

You can delete your Car Nanny account and personal data at any time, either from inside the app or by writing to us.

From the app

  1. Open Car Nanny and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  3. Enter your password to confirm.

Deletion happens immediately. You are signed out and the account can no longer be signed into.

By email

If you have already uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email support@carnanny.app from the address registered to your account, with the subject Delete my account.

We verify the request comes from the account holder and complete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Photos of your vehicle registration and insurance documents
  • Vehicle photos and marketplace listing photos you uploaded
  • AI assistant conversations and support ticket messages
  • Notification preferences and push notification tokens
  • Rewards balance, transaction history and referrals

What is kept, and why

Records of completed bookings, payments and invoices are retained as anonymous records with no link to you personally. We are required to keep financial and tax records for a fixed period, so these cannot be removed on request — but they no longer identify you.

Reviews you left for partner garages are kept as a rating only; any comment text you wrote is removed.

Questions about your data? Contact support@carnanny.app.
